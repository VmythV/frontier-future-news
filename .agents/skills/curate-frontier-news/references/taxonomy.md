# Topic taxonomy

Use exactly three primary categories. Assign one or more only when the news materially advances that topic.

## `agents`

Include autonomous or semi-autonomous AI systems that plan, reason, use tools, maintain memory, coordinate, browse, code, act in environments, or are evaluated as agents.

Include:

- agent architectures, runtimes, protocols, memory, planning, tool use, computer use, multi-agent systems, evaluation, reliability, and safety;
- consequential agent products or open-source releases with technical substance;
- research that materially improves long-horizon or environment-grounded agency.

Exclude:

- ordinary chatbots described as agents only for marketing;
- generic model releases without a demonstrated agent capability;
- minor integrations, funding news, opinion pieces, and listicles without a technical development.

## `embodied-ai`

Include AI whose perception, reasoning, and action are grounded in a physical body or realistic physical environment.

Include:

- humanoids, mobile manipulators, robot learning, vision-language-action models, tactile sensing, locomotion, manipulation, autonomy, sim-to-real, and robotics foundation models;
- meaningful hardware-software co-design, datasets, benchmarks, deployments, and safety results;
- spatial intelligence when it directly supports physical interaction.

Exclude:

- conventional industrial automation with no material AI advance;
- robot product announcements lacking technical evidence;
- autonomous driving unless it contributes a broadly relevant embodied-learning result.

## `world-models`

Include learned representations or simulators that predict, generate, or reason about environment dynamics over space and time.

Include:

- latent dynamics, video or interactive environment generation, neural simulators, predictive control, model-based reinforcement learning, and learned physics;
- world models used for agent planning, robotics, spatial reasoning, or synthetic environment generation;
- datasets and evaluations that materially test temporal consistency, controllability, causality, or physical fidelity.

Exclude:

- generic image or video generation with no environment dynamics, interaction, or planning relevance;
- the phrase "world model" used only as speculation;
- ordinary digital twins without a learned predictive component.

## Cross-topic rules

- Assign multiple categories only when the source demonstrates a real connection, such as a world model used to plan robot actions.
- Prefer tags for adjacent ideas rather than creating new primary categories.
- Use lowercase hyphenated English tags. Recommended tags include `robotics`, `humanoid`, `vla`, `spatial-intelligence`, `simulation`, `reasoning`, `memory`, `tool-use`, `computer-use`, `multi-agent`, `benchmark`, `dataset`, `open-source`, `safety`, and `hardware`.
- Reject a lead when its connection to all three categories depends only on marketing language.
